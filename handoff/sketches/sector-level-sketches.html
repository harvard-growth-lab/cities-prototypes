The map at the sector level: what a block can carry

At the sector level the map is nine blocks, and a block with a name and a share says only what the key says. Nine blocks have room, though. These are five ways of using it, on the real 2024 tiling at the figure's own proportions, each block carrying what its size allows and no more: the biggest sectors say most, Natural Resources says its name. The jobs and shares are the sectors' own; the largest groups are the 4-digit groups inside each; the change is each sector's jobs in 2024 against 2014, a year.

Whichever dressing is chosen, the rule I would adopt with it: the level control sets where the map starts, and the zoom always goes one level finer - a sector opens at 4 digits, a group at 6 - so the three levels are one system and no block ever fills the map alone.
