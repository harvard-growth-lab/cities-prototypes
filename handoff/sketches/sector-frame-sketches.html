The sectors named on the map: four framings

Today the map names industries and the key names the sectors; a sector's block is outlined only while its key entry or its phrase is pointed at. These are four ways of writing the sector's name on the map itself, each on the real 2024 tiling at the figure's own proportions (880 by 500), with the same nine colours. The cost in every case is room: whatever carries the name is taken from the sector's cells, and the smallest sectors pay most.

The band (1) is the most literal frame; the gutter (2) keeps the names in ink on white like the rest of the page's type; the chip (3) costs no room but covers cells; the rule (4) is the lightest touch. In every one a name that will not fit its block at 11px tries its short form, then stands down and leaves the frame alone.
