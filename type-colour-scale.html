Type and colour scale

The type of Who are you? and Metro Industries, and the colours of the whole tool, after the reviews of 2026-10-07. Every interface colour the site draws is one of the twelve below; anything not here is data (the sector palette, the complexity ramp, the maps' shading).

Colour

Twelve colours for the whole tool, defined once in :root: a neutral ramp from the ink to the white, one accent with its tint, a warm accent in two steps, and the two directions; one white for every ground and one tint for every highlighted ground. Text reads at 4.5:1 or better on every ground it sits on; furniture a reader finds by its edge reads at 3:1. Ratios are on white / the old ground / tint.
